What's covered

The same specialist focus, built around you.

These sessions cover the same core areas as the club program — technical assessment, bowling plans, reading batters and game awareness — but with the full hour focused entirely on you and your game.

No write-up after the session. Just focused, practical coaching in real time with specific things to take away and work on.

01

Technical assessment

Analysis of your action with practical, immediate feedback.

02

Bowling plans

Building plans for different batters and match situations.

03

Reading batters

What to look for and how to adjust your plans mid-spell.

04

Game awareness

Building pressure, owning a spell, knowing your best ball under pressure.
