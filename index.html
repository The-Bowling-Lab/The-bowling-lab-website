What is The Bowling Lab

Fast bowling is a specialist skill. It needs specialist coaching.

For clubs, I slot into your existing indoor pre-season sessions and work with your fast bowlers in real time. No extra night required, no disruption to your program. Coaches get a written summary after every session to keep reinforcing it through pre-season.

For individual players and parents, 1:1 and small group sessions are available year-round, outdoors or indoors with nets included, tailored entirely to the bowler's game.

What's included

01

Technical assessment

Individual analysis of each bowler's action with practical feedback they can apply straight away.

02

Bowling plans

How to set up a batter, build pressure, and create wicket-taking opportunities during a spell.

03

Reading batters

Adjusting plans mid-spell based on what the batter is showing you.

04

Game awareness

Building pressure, setting fields, owning a spell. The mental side of fast bowling.

05

Individual feedback

Every bowler leaves with specific things to work on, tailored to their level and style.

06

Written summary for coaches

A full written summary after every session. Travel included.

About the coach

Built by someone who's been on both sides of it.

When I was head coach at Wonga Park, I always wanted to bring someone in specifically for the fast bowlers. A different voice. A specialist in that skill area who might say the same thing a different way, or put it across in a way that actually lands with the group. I couldn't find it, so I built it.

Outside of cricket, I have a background in university lecturing and working with young people, so whether I'm coaching a junior finding their action or a senior bowler sharpening their plans, getting ideas across clearly is something I'm comfortable with.
