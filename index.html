03 · Video Analysis

Specialist feedback, without the full session.

Not every bowler wants to commit to a full session to get started. Video analysis gives you the same specialist eye on your action, using footage you film yourself, no booking required.

01

Filming guide

Clear instructions on what to capture: side-on and rear-angle footage of your full run-up, filmed in landscape with good light.

02

Frame-by-frame breakdown

Your action reviewed in slow motion, with key positions through the run-up, load and release examined in detail.

03

Recorded analysis

A video response talking you through exactly what's being seen, in plain terms, against the footage itself.

04

Priority list

A short, specific list of what to work on first. One or two changes, not ten, so it's something you can actually act on.

$50
Delivered via a private link within 7 days
